# Fees

Addax charges up to **0.1%** of the filled size, in USDC. You approve that maximum once. An order cannot charge more than you approved.

**40% of protocol fees are paid to ADDX stakers.** The rest stays with the protocol. Funding is not a protocol fee. Funding moves between longs and shorts once an hour. See [Funding Rates](funding-rates.md).

A $10,000 fill at 0.1% is a $10 fee. Of a fee taken at the maximum, 40% is the staker share.

Addax is built on [Lighter](https://lighter.xyz/).
