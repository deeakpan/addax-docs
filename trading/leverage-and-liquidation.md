# Leverage & Liquidation

Each market sets its own maximum leverage. BTC and ETH allow up to 50x. Thinner markets allow less.

Initial margin is what you need to open. Maintenance margin is the level at which the position can be liquidated. A stop can close the position before that.

A 50x position changes about 50 times as fast as the index, up or down.
