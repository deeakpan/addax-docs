# Funding Rates

Funding is exchanged once an hour. It keeps the perpetual price near the index. Addax does not take a cut of it, and it is not part of the 40% paid to ADDX stakers.

If the funding rate is positive, longs pay shorts. If it is negative, shorts pay longs. The payment is your position size times the index price times that hour's rate.

The figure shown before the hour ends is an estimate. The rate after the hour is the one that was paid.

Addax is built on [Lighter](https://lighter.xyz/).
